1. Where we deliver

We deliver to every serviceable PIN code in India. We do not ship outside India at present.

2. Delivery charges

Delivery is free on every order, with no minimum order value. The price you see on the product page is the full amount you pay.

3. How long it takes

  • Dispatch: within 1–2 working days after your order is confirmed (for online payments, once the payment is verified). Items sold by other sellers on the Marketplace may take up to 3 working days.
  • Estimated delivery: 3–7 working days, depending on your PIN code. Remote areas can take a little longer.
  • Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.
  • An order with items from more than one seller may arrive in separate parcels.

4. Payment

All orders are prepaid. You can pay by UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), debit or credit card, net banking or wallet through our secure Razorpay checkout. We never see or store your card details or UPI PIN. Cash on delivery is not available at present.

5. Tracking your order

Open indiabusinessinternational.online → Track My Order and enter your order ID and mobile number. Once your parcel is shipped you will see the courier name and tracking (AWB) number there.

6. Damaged, missing or late parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged or an item is missing, tell us within 7 days of delivery with photos — see our Returns & Refunds Policy. If your order has not arrived within the estimated time, contact customer care and we will trace it with the courier.

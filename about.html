Who we are

India Business International (IBI) is an Indian e-commerce company that brings authentic, high-quality Indian products to homes across India. It began in agricultural exports and has grown into a trusted online business with its own brand, iINTELLIGENCEi, and its own store at www.IndiaBusinessInternational.Online.

India Business International is a proprietorship of Dr T Sasimurugan ME.PhD., registered for GST in Tamil Nadu, India, with GSTIN 33AOLPP8759A2ZO. We run the IBI eCommerce Marketplace from Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu.

What we sell

Our range covers Home & Kitchen, Home Improvement, Food & Gourmet, and Textiles. Popular lines include traditional coconut and coir products, handcrafted clay kitchenware, BIS-certified aluminium kitchenware, natural textiles, speciality foods, and herbal and craft supplies. We source from Indian makers and our own farms, and every product reflects India's heritage and craftsmanship.

Other sellers also list on the Marketplace. We also offer business software such as IBI Hospitality PoS, IBI Product Listings Master and IBI Apps.

Where you can buy

IBI sells on 11 platforms: IBI eCommerce Marketplace (our own store), Amazon, Amazon Bazaar, Flipkart, Shopsy, Meesho, JioMart, ShopClues, IndiaMART, Bazaar.in and ONDC (MyStore).

Our founder

IBI was founded in 2019 by Dr T Sasimurugan ME.PhD., Founder, CEO & CTO, who brings long experience in education and administration. He served as Principal of RVS Chennai Engineering Campus and Gojan School of Business & Technology, Chennai, and as Director of Engineering, PRIST University, Chennai ECR Campus. Under his leadership, IBI follows a customer-first, quality-focused model and keeps growing in India's online retail sector.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have. It follows the Information Technology Act 2000 and its rules and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. By using our website or services, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Order & contact details you give when buying or enquiring — name, mobile number, email, delivery address and order contents.
  • Payment details — you pay on Razorpay’s secure page. Your card number, UPI PIN and bank login go to Razorpay, never to us. We receive only the payment ID, the method (for example UPI or card), the amount and whether it succeeded.
  • Email verification — your email address and the one-time code we send to it.
  • Reviews — the rating, text and name you give with a review. Published reviews are shown to everyone with that name.
  • Returns and support — the reason, photos and messages you send when you ask for a return or help.
  • Seller/account information if you register as a seller — business name, owner name, contact details, PAN and GSTIN, registered and pickup address, payout bank account, KYC documents (PAN card, GST certificate, bank proof, address proof) and product listings. KYC documents are stored in a private folder, used only to verify your account and pay you, and are never made public.
  • Usage data — basic, non-identifying information such as the page viewed, the referring site and the device type, used to keep the site working well.
  • Connected social accounts — when we connect our own business accounts (such as Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or Pinterest) to publish our product posts, we store the access tokens needed to post on our own profiles. We do not access your personal social accounts.

2. How we use your information

  • To take payment for, deliver and support your orders, returns and refunds, and to answer your enquiries.
  • To send you order, payment, shipping and refund messages by email or WhatsApp.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and product catalogue, and to prevent fraud.
  • To publish our own product promotions to our own social media profiles.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We use your details only for these purposes, with your consent when you place an order or register, and where the law requires it.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Marketplace:

  • The seller of your item — your name, mobile number and delivery address, for that order only. Sellers may not use it for anything else.
  • Payment provider — Razorpay, to process payments and refunds securely.
  • Courier partners — such as Shiprocket, Delhivery and the couriers they use, to pick up and deliver parcels (name, mobile number, address and parcel details).
  • Service providers — Google (Sheets, Drive, Apps Script and Gmail, where our records are kept and emails are sent), EmailJS (order emails), GitHub Pages and Cloudflare (website hosting and secure payment notifications), and Meta, Pinterest and YouTube only to publish our own posts.
  • Authorities — where required by law.

4. Cookies and local storage

Our website uses essential cookies and your browser’s local storage to remember your cart, preferences and sign-in. We do not use advertising cookies. You can clear them in your browser settings; doing so may empty your cart or sign you out.

5. Data security & retention

We take reasonable security measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

We keep order, payment and invoice records for as long as GST and income-tax law require (under GST, 72 months from the due date of the annual return for that year). Seller KYC documents are kept while the account is open and for the same period afterwards. Other information is deleted when it is no longer needed.

6. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct or update it, or to erase it (except the records the law requires us to keep). You may withdraw your consent at any time; we then stop using your details, except to finish an order already placed or to meet a legal duty. You may also nominate a person to exercise these rights for you. Write to the Grievance Officer below.

7. Third-party links

Our website and posts may link to third-party sites (e.g. our product pages or social platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties; please review their policies.

8. Children

You must be 18 or older to place an order or open an account. A person under 18 may use the website only with a parent or guardian, who places the order. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 without verifiable parental consent, and we do not track children or target advertising at them.

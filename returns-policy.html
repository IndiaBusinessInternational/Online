3. How to request a return

  1. Open indiabusinessinternational.online → Your Orders or Track My Order, and choose Request a return on the order. Add the reason and photos of the item and the parcel.
  2. Or WhatsApp us on +91 89394 14799 or email indiabusinessinternational@gmail.com with your order ID and photos.
  3. Once the return is approved, pack the item in its original condition; the courier or seller will contact you for pickup.

4. Refunds

  • We refund once the returned item reaches the seller and is checked. You will get an email when the refund is issued.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method — the same UPI ID, card, bank account or wallet you paid with.
  • After we issue the refund, banks usually credit it within 5–7 working days (UPI is often faster).

5. Cancelling an order

You can cancel any time before the order is dispatched: WhatsApp or call customer care with your order ID and you get a full refund to your original payment method. If a seller cannot fulfil your order, we cancel it and refund you in full. After dispatch, use the return process above.
